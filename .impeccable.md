## Design Context

### Users

This is a personal Neovim environment for one keyboard-first developer who moves between software work and prose. The editor should support dense coding workflows and focused writing without requiring separate setups.

### Brand Personality

Quietly capable, personal, and deliberate. The experience should feel calm at rest and confidently expressive when navigating, debugging, or reviewing work.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use Material deep ocean as a dark, warm-tinted base. Keep persistent chrome sparse and legible; reserve richer colour, motion, and contextual detail for active states and deliberate interactions.

### Design Principles

- Prioritize a calm editing canvas over constant visual stimulation.
- Reveal workflow detail contextually instead of showing all metadata at once.
- Use the warm accent colour for focus, state, and meaningful feedback.
- Keep motion subtle, optional, and purposeful.
- Prefer improving or consolidating existing tools before adding plugins.
